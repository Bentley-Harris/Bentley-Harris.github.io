Camping Cooktop

2025 · Fabrication & Manufacturing
  • Welding
  • Lathe
  • Technical drawing

A camp cooktop I designed and built from scratch: a welded steel frame with a barred grill and a solid plate section, on legs that bolt through machined pivots. The whole thing was drawn up first as a dimensioned plan, then built to that drawing.

Key details

  • Produced a full dimensioned drawing before cutting anything, with lettered detail views for every welded joint, the axle, the lock bolt and the leg pivots.
  • Welded the frame and cooking surface from mild steel square stock, with round bar for the grill section and plate for the solid cooking surface.
  • Machined the leg pivot components on a lathe, reducing round stock to size for the bushed joints.
  • Built the leg pivots as bolted assemblies with welded washer seats, so the legs fold against the body for transport.
