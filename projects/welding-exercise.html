Welding Exercise

2025 · Additional Manufacturing
  • Welding
  • Fillet welds
  • Joint preparation

Welding practice on mild steel plate: corner joints and tee fillets, taken from tacked setup through to finished runs. The point of the exercise was consistency: keeping the bead profile and travel speed even along the full length of each joint.

Key details

  • Prepared and tacked the joints square before running the full welds.
  • Ran fillet welds on tee joints, working on even bead width and consistent travel speed.
  • Welded a continuous bead around an outside corner, keeping the profile through the direction changes.
  • Chipped and inspected each run, then repeated the joint to improve on the last attempt.
