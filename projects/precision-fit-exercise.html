Precision Fit Exercise

2024 · Additional Manufacturing
  • Hand finishing
  • Filing
  • Layout

A manufacturing exercise in getting flat steel pieces to fit each other precisely. Each piece was marked out, cut and then hand-finished until the set interlocks with a consistent, tight seam on every mating edge.

Key details

  • Marked out the profiles on flat steel plate and cut each piece to shape.
  • Hand-finished every mating edge with files until the pieces slid together without force.
  • Drilled relief holes at the internal corners so the fitted edges could seat fully.
  • Brought the faces to a uniform brushed finish.
