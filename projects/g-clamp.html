G-Clamp

2025 · Fabrication & Manufacturing
  • Metal lathe
  • Threading
  • Welding

A working G-clamp fabricated from raw steel stock as the major practical assessment for my Certificate II in Engineering, built to a formal engineering drawing pack. The screw and pad assembly was the interesting part: a clamp is only useful if the thread runs true and the pad stays flat against the work as it tightens, and both of those depend on the machining rather than on the frame.

Key details

  • Built to a formal engineering drawing pack — reading the drawings, parts list and tolerances was part of the assessment.
