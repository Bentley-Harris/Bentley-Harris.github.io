Sheet Metal Toolbox

2024 · Fabrication & Manufacturing
  • Sheet metal
  • Layout
  • Forming

A toolbox made from flat sheet: laid out, cut, folded and joined. Sheet metal is unforgiving of a bad layout, because everything downstream depends on the flat pattern being right before a single fold is made. Get the development wrong and you find out at the last bend.

Key details

  • Developed the flat pattern from the finished dimensions before cutting anything.
  • Cut, formed and joined the sheet into the finished box.
  • Made from 0.5 mm Galvabond sheet.
