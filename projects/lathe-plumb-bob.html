Lathe-Machined Plumb Bob

2025 · Additional Manufacturing
  • Lathe
  • Turning
  • Measurement

A machining exercise: a stepped plumb bob turned from round steel bar on a metal lathe, working to a dimensioned drawing. The part steps down through several diameters and finishes in a turned point, with each diameter checked against the drawing as it was cut.

Key details

  • Worked from a dimensioned machining drawing, marking off each feature as it was completed.
  • Cut the blank from bar stock on a bandsaw, then faced and centre-drilled it in the lathe.
  • Turned the stepped diameters between the chuck and a tailstock centre, and turned the point to finish.
  • Used a digital readout on the lathe for positioning, and checked diameters with vernier calipers against the drawing.
