Rocket Stove

2024 · Fabrication & Manufacturing
  • Welding
  • Sheet metal
  • Angle grinder

A rocket stove built from large hollow square bar during my Certificate II in Engineering. Wood feeds into the horizontal section at the base and the flame exits through the vertical riser at the top, which draws the fire through the elbow and burns it hot. The design is simple; making it out of heavy section without warping it is the part that takes work.

Key details

  • Formed from large hollow square bar into a J-tube: horizontal feed at the base, vertical riser above.
  • Cut and welded the sections to form the internal path.
  • Fabricated a custom trapdoor closure for the feed opening.
