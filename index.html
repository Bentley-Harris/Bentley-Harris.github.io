Bentley Harris

Engineering student working in fabrication, electronics and embedded systems.

I am a first-year electrical engineering student at the University of Queensland, studying a combined Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) and Master of Engineering. Before university I completed a two-year Certificate II in Engineering during high school. That course is where I learned to weld, run a lathe, work sheet metal and heat treat steel, and it produced most of the fabrication work on this site.

Outside coursework I design and build electronics. I started with the basics of driving fans and motors, controlling LEDs and reading sensors, then moved on to complete builds: Arduino control systems, PID stabilisation, hand-soldered perfboard circuits laid out in KiCad, and enclosures I model in CAD and print myself. The largest project I have worked on is a self-stabilising floating house, built with a university team, and it taught me most of what I know about tuning a control loop.

I document everything I build. That is what this site is for.

This site is a work in progress. Some projects are not fully written up yet, and I am adding more detail to them over time.
